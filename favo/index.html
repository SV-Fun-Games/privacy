Google’s ads SDK, which runs inside the game, may automatically collect:

  • your device’s advertising ID and app set ID;
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location may be estimated (city or region, never your exact location);
  • device and system information, such as model, Android version, language and connection type;
  • interactions with ads, such as impressions, taps and videos watched;
  • diagnostic and performance data about the SDK itself.

This data is used to serve and measure ads, limit how often the same ad repeats, prevent fraud and abuse and, only with your consent where the law requires it, show personalized ads. We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you: Google processes it under its own policies:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

  • With Google: you can also manage personalization in My Ad Center.

Without consent to personalization, Google may still show non-personalized ads, which use limited data for delivery, measurement and fraud prevention.
